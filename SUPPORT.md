## English

This project is the [G316 X 98 community plugin for SignalRGB](https://github.com/purrk0t/g316x-signalrgb-plugin). It is free and open source. If the plugin helped you and you would like to support continued hardware testing, compatibility work, documentation, and upstreaming efforts, optional tips are welcome.

**Tips are entirely voluntary.** They are not required to use the plugin and do not purchase software, warranty, priority support, or additional functionality.
